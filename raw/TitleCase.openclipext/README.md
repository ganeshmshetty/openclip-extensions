# Title Case

Capitalize selected text according to English title conventions.

Select a headline, phrase, or title and choose **Title Case** from OpenClip to format it in standard English title case.

## Features

- **Headline Capitalization**: Capitalizes major words while keeping articles, conjunctions, and minor prepositions lowercase (unless at the start or end).
- **Fast Local Execution**: Executes instantly on your Mac via JavaScript.

## Usage Examples

- `the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog` → `The Quick Brown Fox Jumps Over the Lazy Dog`
- `war and peace` → `War and Peace`

## Requirements

- OpenClip 1.0.0 or later.
