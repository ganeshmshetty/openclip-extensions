# Long Date

Paste today's date written out in full at the cursor, for example **Sunday, 25 October 2026**.

## Features

- **Your language and region**: follows your Mac's locale, so you get "Sunday, October 25, 2026" in the US, "dimanche 25 octobre 2026" in French or "2026年10月25日日曜日" in Japanese.
- **Paste or copy**: click to paste; right-click or ⇧-click to copy instead.
- **No selection needed**: works anywhere you can type.

## Requirements

- OpenClip 1.1.0 or later.
