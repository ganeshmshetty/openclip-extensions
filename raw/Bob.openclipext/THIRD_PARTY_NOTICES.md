`icon.svg` is a monochrome vector adaptation of the Bob icon distributed by Bob's author, ripperhe, in the official Bob–PopClip integration. `icon-source.png` is the unmodified source image, included for attribution and reproducibility.

- Original image: [source/Bob.png at a756b5040e437dee5d96846dbe55caeb138b654c](https://github.com/ripperhe/bob-popclip/blob/a756b5040e437dee5d96846dbe55caeb138b654c/source/Bob.png).

- Adaptation by Leon (s010s), 2026-09-08: traced the alpha silhouette to editable vector paths, simplified the contour, and used a square viewBox with `fill="currentColor"`. No raster image is embedded in `icon.svg`.
