# Smart Quotes

Switch selected text between straight quotes (`"` `'`) and curly quotes (“ ” ‘ ’).

## Actions

- **Curly Quotes**: turns `"Hello," she said. It's fine.` into “Hello,” she said. It’s fine. Apostrophes in words like *don’t* and years like *’90s* come out right.
- **Straight Quotes**: turns curly quotes and primes back into plain `"` and `'`, handy before pasting into code, terminals or plain-text fields.

Each action only shows up when the selection has quotes it can convert.

## Requirements

- OpenClip 1.1.0 or later.
