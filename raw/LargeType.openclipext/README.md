# Large Type

Display any selected text large across your entire display in a beautiful, edge-to-edge floating overlay.

Inspired by classic macOS Large Type utilities, this extension covers your display with a dark frosted glass backdrop and renders the selected text front-and-center in giant typography—perfect for phone numbers, verification codes, Wi-Fi passwords, addresses, or reading across a room.

## Features

- **Full Display Coverage**: Seamlessly dims and blurs your entire screen edge-to-edge behind a dark frosted glass overlay.
- **Giant Adaptive Typography**: Dynamically scales the font up to 130pt for short codes or down to 26pt for paragraphs, keeping it centered.
- **Instant Dismissal**: Click anywhere on the screen, or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, <kbd>Space</kbd>, or <kbd>Return</kbd> to dismiss immediately.
- **Scrollable Support**: If the text exceeds the screen height, it smoothly scrolls without losing the full display backdrop.
- **Zero Dependencies**: Lightweight native macOS AppKit universal binary (`arm64` + `x86_64`) that launches in milliseconds with zero permissions required.

## Usage

1. Highlight any text in any app.
2. Trigger **Large Type** in OpenClip.
3. The text fills the center of your screen over a dimmed translucent backdrop.
4. Click anywhere or press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to return to your work.
